VOLUME I -- TECHNICAL CAPABILITY

Summit Biomedical Field Services demonstrates the following approach to meeting the
requirements of the Statement of Work (SOW) and this solicitation, addressing the SOW
task sequence directly, section by section, and consistent with the Quality Assurance
Surveillance Plan.

Infection Control: Summit will establish and maintain a full Infection Control Risk
Assessment (ICRA) Level IV compliant work area surrounding the Radiology suite for the
duration of the project, including dust barriers and any protective measures directed by
the facility's Infection Prevention and/or Safety offices, and will implement continuous
debris control, cleaning, and containment measures throughout de-installation and
removal.

Floor Area: Upon completion of system removal, Summit will remove all anchoring bolts
and other hardware associated with the GE INNOVA 4100-IQ installation from the floor.

Field Service Report: Upon completion of work, Summit will submit a field service report
to Biomedical Engineering and the COR documenting the equipment removed (device name
and model) and the dates of on-site work.

Personnel: The lead Field Service Engineer assigned to this project, Daniel Reyes, has
5 years of hands-on field experience de-installing and maintaining GE imaging systems and
holds current OEM de-installation certification.

Safety: Summit's personnel wear appropriate personal protective equipment on all job
sites in accordance with OSHA requirements.
