Standards Compliance:
Our work will be performed in accordance with AWWA C652 (disinfection),
AWWA D101/M42 (inspection and maintenance of steel tanks), applicable
NHDES administrative rules for public water systems, OSHA 1910.146
(permit-required confined spaces), and EPA guidance on potable water
system disinfection byproducts.

Inspection Methodology:
Our inspection approach begins with a topside visual survey, followed
by interior drain-down and manual diver-assisted visual inspection of
the tank floor, walls, and roof structure. We document coating
condition, corrosion, and sediment buildup with photographs at each
station, followed by a written verification checklist and a
sediment-removal and disinfection sequence per AWWA C652. Our
methodology relies on manual diver-assisted visual inspection; we do
not currently utilize ROV (remotely operated vehicle) technology for
submerged surface inspection.

Detailed operational procedures and task-sequencing work plans for this
project are available upon request and will be finalized in
coordination with the COR once the work schedule is confirmed.

Structural and Defect Assessment:
Our inspectors are trained to identify structural defects, coating
failures, corrosion, erosion, sanitation deficiencies, and
contamination routes. A sample inspection report from a comparable
municipal tank project is available upon request, illustrating our
defect-classification and reporting format.
