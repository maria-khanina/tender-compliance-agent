VOLUME I -- TECHNICAL CAPABILITY

Continental demonstrates the following approach to meeting the requirements of the
Statement of Work (SOW) and this solicitation.

Contract Vehicle: The items and services quoted are not currently available or priced
under any Government-wide Acquisition Contract (GWAC), GSA Multiple Award Schedule
(MAS), or VA-wide Acquisition Contract; this quote is submitted as an open-market offer.

Infection Control: Continental will establish and maintain a full Infection Control Risk
Assessment (ICRA) Level IV compliant work area surrounding the Radiology suite for the
duration of the project, including dust barriers and any protective measures directed by
the facility's Infection Prevention and/or Safety offices, with continuous debris control,
cleaning, and containment measures throughout de-installation and removal.

Floor Area: Upon completion of system removal, Continental will remove all anchoring
bolts and other hardware associated with the GE INNOVA 4100-IQ installation from the
floor, and will remove all resulting trash and debris from the site.

Field Service Report: Upon completion of work, Continental will submit a detailed field
service report to Biomedical Engineering and the COR identifying the equipment removed
(device name and model), the dates and times on site, a description of the tasks
performed, confirmation of the monitor arm's final disposition, and a description of any
issues encountered along with the corrective actions taken.

Personnel: The lead Field Service Engineer assigned to this project, Priya Desai, has
18 months of hands-on field experience de-installing and maintaining GE imaging systems
and holds current OEM de-installation training.

Safety: Continental's Bloodborne Pathogens Exposure Control Plan educates employees on
bloodborne pathogen risks during equipment removal, implements exposure-reduction
procedures including engineering and work-practice controls, provides post-exposure
counseling and treatment referral through our occupational health provider, and requires
and enforces PPE use by all field personnel on every job site.
