Cylinder Tracking:
For cylinders in transit to and from ordering activities, we will use a
barcode-based tracking system (method: handheld barcode scanners at
pickup and delivery, data stored in a cloud portal) that allows ordering
activity POCs on-demand access to view and extract delivery and location
data for all cylinders moving in and out of their facility.

We do not currently operate a system for tracking medical gas cylinders
within an ordering activity's facility after delivery, and are therefore
not submitting a facility-side tracking plan with this proposal.

Reporting Capabilities:
Northern Plains Medical Gases will provide on-demand usage, cost, and
cylinder-tracking reports through the same cloud portal referenced above,
covering actual and average deliveries by week, month, and year and by
gas type; actual and average billing data by week, month, and year per
medical gas; and perpetual delivery, pick-up, and location data per
cylinder. Reports will be viewable through the web portal or exported to
an Excel spreadsheet upon request.
