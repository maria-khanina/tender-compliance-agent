Specifically, alternate systems reviewed cannot be modified to meet the minimum needs of VA Central Western Massachusetts (CWM) without introducing unacceptable integration risk, operational disruption, and patient-care impact.

Site-specific interoperability cannot be assured. Alternate brands require full revalidation of connectivity and workflow performance across the site’s PACS, VNA, RIS, and EMR systems. Patient-care risk cannot be mitigated.
