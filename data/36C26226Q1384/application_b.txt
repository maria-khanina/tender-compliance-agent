Technical Description: The AquaLine 4H-Pro is an air-driven, high speed
(4-hole) system fitting Midwest 4-hole handpieces, using autoclavable
tubing for a sterile delivery pathway. The kit includes one 16 ounce
autoclavable bottle rather than the standard two-bottle configuration,
providing equivalent total irrigation capacity in a single reservoir.
The waterline system detaches fully from the handpiece body for
independent sterilization. The full unit is described as fully
autoclavable to ensure sterility between uses, and it is designed to
provide high-speed cutting power rivaling electric handpieces while
maintaining precision and responsiveness. Irrigation is controlled with
a simple instant on/off mechanism and no added footprint. The AquaLine
4H-Pro integrates seamlessly with Midwest, DentalEZ, and A-dec delivery
systems without adapters, covering the majority of dental units in use
today.

Exhibit B Sterilization Compatibility Chart excerpt: "Note: the
