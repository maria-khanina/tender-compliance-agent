B.3 PERFORMANCE WORK STATEMENT 
GREASE TRAP SERVICES 
Contractor shall provide all labor, materials, equipment, and tools necessary to clean grease traps and to 
dispose of grease. Grease traps are located at/in Building 424 (in-patient dining facility), 405, 409, 410, 
411 and 412 at the Tomah VAMC, Tomah WI., according to this Performance Work Statement. 
1. SCOPE 
A. Equipment to be covered under this contract: 
1. One (1) 2000 Gallon Grease Trap (Located in Building 424) 
2. Two (2) 30 Gallon Grease Traps (Buildings 409 & 410) 
3. Two (2) 17 Gallon Grease Traps (Buildings 411 & 412) 
4. One (1) 240 Gallon Grease Trap (Building 405) 
 
B. Provide six (6) service inspections per contract year, to occur in the months of December, 
February, April, June, August, and October. Each service inspection shall require contractor to: 
1. Remove and dispose of grease per all Federal, State and Local regulations. 
2. Clean grease traps after removal of grease is completed. 
 
C. Provide up to three (3) service calls per contract year to pump lift station located in Building 
410.

Per Amendment 0001, Vendor Questions and Answers:
2. “Please clarify the disposal requirement. The description states that the contractor shall 
“dispose of grease at facilities located at the Tomah VA Medical Center. ” Does the Tomah 
VA provide an approved onsite disposal or receiving facility for grease-trap waste? If so, 
please identify the disposal location, procedures, operating hours, capacity restrictions, 
and any applicable fees. If not, please conﬁrm that the contractor must transport the waste 
to an offsite licensed receiving facility and include all transportation and disposal costs in 
its price.” 
The Tomah VA does not provide an approved onsite disposal or receiving facility for 
grease-trap waste. The contractor is responsible for transporting the waste to an 
offsite location. Please refer to the Performance Work Statement (Section B.3) in the 
solicitation.

3. “Please provide the number, location, type, rated capacity, approximate current 
contents, and required cleaning frequency of each grease trap or interceptor. Please also 
specify required service documentation, including disposal manifests, volume tickets, 
photographs, and inspection reports.” 
Refer to Section B.3 Performance Work Statement for building locations, cleaning 
frequency, and grease trap sizes. “Current content” in the grease traps is grease. The 
government does not require service documentation/reports upon completion of 
service.
