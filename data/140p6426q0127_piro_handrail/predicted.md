**REQUIREMENT:** The company must be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM) with an active status at the time of quote/proposal submission, at the time of award, and throughout the entire award/contract performance, and must be considered a small business for the size standard associated with the NAICS code found in block 10 on page one of the solicitation.

**Reasoning:** The application contains contradictory statements regarding the bidder's status as a small business. In chunk [2], the bidder states that its average annual gross revenue over the past three fiscal years is $12.4 million, which is within the $19 million small business size standard for NAICS code 238990. However, in chunk [3], the bidder states that its average annual revenue for size-standard purposes in fiscal year 2024 was $21.8 million, which exceeds the $19 million small business size standard.

**Evidence:**

company's registration in the System for Award Management (SAM) is active and will remain
active through quote submission, award, and the entire period of contract performance.
- (supports) Superior Rail & Fabrication, LLC has maintained average annual gross revenue of $12.4
million over the past three fiscal years, comfortably within the $19 million small business
size standard associated with NAICS code 238990.
- (contradicts) For purposes of confirming our financial capacity, we note that in fiscal year 2024 Superior
Rail & Fabrication, LLC's average annual revenue for size-standard purposes was $21.8
million, computed across our fabrication and installation divisions.
