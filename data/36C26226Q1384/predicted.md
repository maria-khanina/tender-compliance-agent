**Reasoning:** The application contains contradictory statements regarding whether the items are guaranteed to be sourced through authorized channels to prevent gray market goods. On one hand, the bidder claims to be an authorized distributor with full OEM warranty and service backing, which supports non-gray market status. On the other hand, the bidder explicitly states that orders may be fulfilled using units obtained through a secondary distribution network including units allocated for other regional markets, which introduces the risk of gray market or diverted goods and directly conflicts with the verification that items are not gray market.

**Evidence:**

HydroClear product line, verified directly through the manufacturer's
official partner program, with full OEM warranty and service backing.
- (contradicts) Due to current inventory constraints on
this specific model, please note that this order may be fulfilled
using units obtained through a secondary distribution network, which
can include units originally allocated for other regional markets.
