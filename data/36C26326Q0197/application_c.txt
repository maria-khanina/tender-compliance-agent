Cylinder Tracking:
For cylinders in transit to and from ordering activities, we will use a
web-based portal (method: barcode scanning at pickup and delivery,
Microsoft Excel export available) that allows ordering activity POCs
on-demand access to view or extract data related to all cylinders
moving in and out of their facility, including access limits for
Government employees and no requirement for connection to the
Government network.

We do not currently operate a system for tracking medical gas cylinders
within an ordering activity's facility after delivery, and are
therefore not submitting a facility-side tracking plan with this
proposal.

Reporting Capabilities:
Prairie Wind Medical Gases will provide on-demand usage, cost, and
cylinder-tracking reports through the same web-based portal referenced
above, covering actual and average deliveries by week, month, and year
and by gas type; actual and average billing data; and perpetual
delivery, pick-up, and location data per cylinder. Reports will be
viewable through the web portal or exported to Excel.
