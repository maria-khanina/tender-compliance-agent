Standards Compliance:
Our work will be performed in accordance with AWWA C652, AWWA D101/M42,

on potable water system disinfection byproducts.

Inspection Methodology:
Our inspection approach uses a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) for
submerged surface inspection, followed by interior drain-down and
manual verification of the tank floor, walls, and roof structure,
documented with photographs at each station and a written verification
checklist.

Detailed work plans and operational procedures for this project are
included as Attachment B to this quote.

Structural and Defect Assessment:
Our inspectors are trained to identify structural defects, coating
failures, corrosion, erosion, sanitation deficiencies, and
contamination routes. A sample inspection report from a comparable
municipal tank project is included as Attachment C.
