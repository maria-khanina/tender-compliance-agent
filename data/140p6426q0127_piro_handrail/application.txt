CAPABILITIES STATEMENT / PROJECT NARRATIVE

Superior Rail & Fabrication, LLC has maintained average annual gross revenue of $12.4
million over the past three fiscal years, comfortably within the $19 million small business
size standard associated with NAICS code 238990.

Our company specializes in the fabrication and installation of steel, aluminum, and
stainless-steel handrail systems for federal, state, and municipal facilities, including
compliance with applicable ADA and local building code requirements. Our crews are equipped
to perform site measurement, custom fabrication in our own shop, and on-site installation,
including all mobilization, demobilization, supplies, and labor described in Item 0010 of the
Schedule of Supplies. Shop drawings and product data sheets for the proposed handrail system
are included as Attachment A to this quote package.

For portions of the handrail hardware, we intend to substitute equivalent stainless-steel
fasteners from an alternate manufacturer other than the brand specified in the Statement of
Work, in order to avoid current supply delays with the named manufacturer.
