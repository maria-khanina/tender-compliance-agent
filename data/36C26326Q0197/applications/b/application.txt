Cylinder Tracking:
For cylinders in transit to and from ordering activities, we will use a
web-based portal (method: barcode scanning at pickup and delivery,
Microsoft Excel export available) that allows ordering activity POCs
on-demand access to view or extract data related to all cylinders
moving in and out of their facility, including access limits for
Government employees and no requirement for connection to the
Government network.

We currently operate a proprietary facility-side cylinder tracking
system for our existing hospital-system clients, which we plan to make
available to VISN 23 ordering activities as well following award.
