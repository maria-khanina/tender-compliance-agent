# Tender Compliance Report

## Mandatory Requirements

### Non-compliant

**REQUIREMENT:** The contractor shall provide all labor, materials, equipment, and tools necessary to clean grease traps, remove and dispose of grease in accordance with all Federal, State, and Local regulations, and include all transportation and offsite disposal costs in the price.

**Reasoning:** The requirement explicitly states that the contractor must include all transportation and offsite disposal costs in the price. The bidder's application fails to provide pricing for the option periods, stating it will be provided upon request after the notice of intent to award. This violates the requirement to include these costs in the bid price.

**Evidence:**
- (contradicts) Option Periods 1-4: Pricing for the option periods will be provided upon request
following any notice of intent to award, once current fuel and disposal-market rates
are confirmed.

### Non-compliant

**REQUIREMENT:** The offeror must provide the number, location, type, rated capacity, approximate current contents, and required cleaning frequency of each grease trap or interceptor.

**Reasoning:** The requirement mandates that the offeror provide the number, location, type, rated capacity, approximate current contents, and required cleaning frequency for each grease trap. While the bidder identifies the number of grease traps (five) and their general locations (Buildings 424, 405, 409, 410, 411, and 412), they fail to provide the type, rated capacity, approximate current contents, or the required cleaning frequency for any of the units.

**Evidence:**
- (contradicts) Northwoods Septic & Grease Solutions, LLC will perform all six annual service
inspections in accordance with the Performance Work Statement, servicing all five
grease traps at Buildings 424, 405, 409, 410, 411, and 412

### Non-compliant

**REQUIREMENT:** The offeror must specify required service documentation, including disposal manifests, volume tickets, photographs, and inspection reports.

**Reasoning:** The requirement explicitly mandates that the offeror must specify required service documentation, including disposal manifests, volume tickets, photographs, and inspection reports. The bidder's application describes the services to be performed but fails to mention or commit to providing the specified documentation (disposal manifests, volume tickets, photographs, and inspection reports) as part of their service delivery.

**Evidence:**
- (inconclusive) Northwoods Septic & Grease Solutions, LLC will perform all six annual service
inspections in accordance with the Performance Work Statement, servicing all five
grease traps at Buildings 424, 405, 409, 410, 411, and 412, and will pump the lift
station at Building 410 up to three times per contract year as required.

### Non-compliant

**REQUIREMENT:** The offeror shall submit information regarding contracts providing similar services, including contract number, size/scope, total years of experience, and the period of performance, particularly for VA facility experience.

**Reasoning:** The requirement asks for the bidder's own experience regarding contracts providing similar services. The bidder provided experience for a subcontractor, but explicitly stated in their technical capability section that they will not use subcontractors for this contract. Furthermore, the bidder failed to provide any information regarding their own company's years of experience or past contract performance, which is a mandatory requirement.

**Evidence:**
- (contradicts) Our subcontractor, Coulee Region Grease Traps Inc., performed grease trap cleaning and
lift station pumping services for three years (2022-2025) under a similar contract at
the William S. Middleton Memorial Veterans Hospital in Madison, WI (contract number
36C25722F0091), servicing four grease traps of comparable size and scope to those at
the Tomah VAMC.
- (supports) All work under this contract will be performed exclusively by Northwoods Septic &
Grease Solutions, LLC's own certified technicians; no subcontractors will be used at
any point in the performance of this contract.
