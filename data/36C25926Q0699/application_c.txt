VOLUME I -- TECHNICAL CAPABILITY

Meridian Biomedical Decommissioning demonstrates the following approach to
meeting the requirements of the Statement of Work (SOW) and this solicitation.

Contract Vehicle: The items and services quoted are not currently available
under any GWAC, GSA Multiple Award Schedule, or VA-wide Acquisition Contract;
this quote is submitted as an open-market offer.

Infection Control: Meridian will establish and maintain a full Infection
Control Risk Assessment (ICRA) Level IV compliant work area surrounding the
Radiology suite for the duration of the project, including dust barriers and
continuous debris control, cleaning, and containment measures throughout
de-installation and removal.

Floor Area: Upon completion of system removal, Meridian will remove all
anchoring bolts and other hardware from the floor, restore the floor to a
flush condition suitable for future construction, and remove all resulting
trash and debris.

Field Service Report: Upon completion of work, Meridian will submit a field
service report to Biomedical Engineering and the COR documenting the
equipment removed (device name and model), the dates of on-site work, a
description of tasks performed, confirmation of the monitor arm's final
disposition, and any issues encountered with corrective actions taken.

Personnel: The lead Field Service Engineer assigned to this project, Carlos
Mendez, has 6 years of hands-on field experience de-installing and
maintaining GE imaging systems and holds current OEM de-installation
certification.

Safety: Meridian's Bloodborne Pathogens Exposure Control Plan educates
employees on bloodborne pathogen risks, implements exposure-reduction
procedures, provides post-exposure counseling and treatment referral, and
requires and enforces PPE use by all field personnel.
